- Replaced the multi-form feature workflow with autonomous specification-driven delivery on 2026-08-02 at the Founder/Product Owner's direction. A direct bounded feature/fix/docs/chore request now authorizes safe specification refinement, reversible product/technical defaults, implementation, local synthetic verification, in-scope correction, separate-agent review, evidence updates, scoped staging/commit, task-branch push, a review-ready pull request, and CI follow-up through green. The routine human decision is merge. Mandatory per-feature Founder/Product Owner decision forms, local acceptance prompts, publication prompts, copy-ready continuation prompts, always-on proximity/outlook reporting, and blanket qualified-human review gates no longer apply to current or future unmerged work. Quality controls remain: bounded branches, deny-by-default tenant security, protected authority, mandatory audit, negative/cross-tenant tests, final matrices, secret/scope scans, and separate-agent review. Hard stops remain for irreducible product/architecture conflict; unsupported aviation/legal/operational authority; credential/restricted-data exposure; cross-tenant/fail-open or missing-audit behavior; destructive or uncertain cleanup; new paid providers/material recurring costs; hosted mutations; external messages to real recipients; real-data use; merge; production; destructive Git/data actions; and branch deletion. Qualified-human validation remains risk-triggered for regulated, legal, aviation, real-data, hosted, production, penetration-test, or formal-compliance claims that inherently require it. Historical FEAT-002 and early FEAT-003 approval records remain evidence of their original process but do not reintroduce those superseded gates.
